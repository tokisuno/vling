                                                                        *vling*
This is a plugin geared towards Humanities students, specifically those
pursuing Linguistics/Cogsci degrees, and are also nerds using Neovim.

This plugin comes configured with a large table of both Deadkeys and IPA
symbols that are commonly used. I've also included other symbols for more
uncommonly used sounds as well.

 vim:tw=78:ts=8:ft=help:norl:
